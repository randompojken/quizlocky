An Opal alternative that makes you actually study

Opal is a genuinely good app blocker. But for students it has a gap: when your apps are blocked, nothing happens next. You're just… blocked. QuizLocky fills that gap by making the unlock productive — you pass a quick quiz built from your own study notes, and that earns your screen time.

What Opal is

Opal is a focus app for iPhone that blocks distracting apps during timed sessions. You schedule focus time, your chosen apps go dark, and Opal tracks how much focus time you banked. It's built for professionals guarding deep-work hours as much as for students.

Common questions

Does QuizLocky block apps like Opal does?

Yes. QuizLocky uses Apple's Screen Time framework, the same system-level blocking Opal relies on. The difference is what unlocks them: in QuizLocky you pass a quiz generated from your own study material instead of waiting out a timer.

Can I use QuizLocky and Opal together?

Technically yes, since both use Screen Time, but for most students one is enough. If your goal is studying more, QuizLocky covers the blocking and the studying in one app.
