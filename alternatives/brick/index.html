A Brick alternative that doesn't cost extra (and teaches you something)

Brick's idea is clever: a physical block you tap your phone against to lock and unlock your apps. The friction is real, because the device lives at home. But it costs real money on top of your phone, and walking to a piece of plastic teaches you nothing. QuizLocky creates its friction with knowledge instead: pass a quiz from your own notes and you've earned your screen time.

What Brick is

Brick is a small physical NFC device paired with an iPhone app. Tap your phone on the Brick to lock your distracting apps; tap again to unlock. Because you can leave the Brick at home, the friction of unlocking is physical distance.

Common questions

Is a software lock as strong as Brick's physical one?

Brick's separation-based friction is very strong. QuizLocky's lock uses Apple's Screen Time framework and is genuinely enforced — but its philosophy is different: make the unlock productive rather than merely difficult. For students, ten minutes of recall beats a walk to the kitchen.

Do I need to buy anything to use QuizLocky?

No. It's just an app: download, pick the apps to lock, add your notes, done in a few minutes.

What if I have an exam and need my phone free?

You control which apps are locked and can adjust your setup at any time — QuizLocky locks the distracting apps you chose, not your whole phone.
