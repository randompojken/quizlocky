A Forest alternative with real teeth (and real studying)

Forest is charming: you plant a virtual tree, and it grows while you leave your phone alone. But its power is guilt, not a lock — on iPhone it mostly can't stop you opening TikTok, and it has nothing to do with your actual coursework. QuizLocky replaces guilt with a deal: the apps stay genuinely locked until you pass a quiz built from your own notes.

What Forest is

Forest is a gamified focus timer. You set a session, a tree grows, and quitting the session kills the tree. It's cross-platform, inexpensive, and its partner program plants real trees — a lovely product for gentle motivation.

Common questions

Is QuizLocky just Forest with quizzes?

No — the mechanics are opposite. Forest asks you to resist your phone while a timer runs. QuizLocky assumes you won't resist, and puts a quiz between you and the apps, so the moment of weakness becomes the moment you study.

Does QuizLocky really lock apps on iPhone?

Yes. It uses Apple's Screen Time framework, so chosen apps genuinely won't open until you clear the quiz. Forest on iPhone is primarily a timer and can't enforce a hard block in the same way.
