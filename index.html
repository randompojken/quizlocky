Why it works

Built on how memory actually works.

QuizLocky isn't a gimmick. It's built around two of the most well-established findings in learning science.

Active recall

Testing yourself is one of the most effective ways to learn — far more than re-reading or highlighting. Every unlock makes you recall, not just review.

Spaced repetition

Reviewing at increasing intervals beats cramming and fights the forgetting curve. Locky resurfaces what you're about to forget.

Protected focus

Phone distraction fragments attention. Locking your apps while you study protects both your time and your memory.
