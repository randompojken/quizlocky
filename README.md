# QuizLocky — earn your screen time

QuizLocky is an iOS app that locks the apps that distract you (TikTok,
Instagram, Snapchat, whichever you pick) until you pass a quiz built from
your **own study notes**. Study 10 minutes, earn 20 minutes of scrolling.

- Paste notes, import a PDF, or snap a photo — Locky's AI writes the quiz
- Apps stay locked via Apple Screen Time until you clear it
- Active recall + spaced repetition, so the studying actually sticks
